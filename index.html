Sourdough with two wild cultures

Naturally leavened bread from Marietta, Georgia, raised by a sourdough starter and a raisin yeast water working together.

The starter

Fed on white and stone-ground wheat. It brings the tang and the structure.

The raisin yeast water

Raisins steeped until they ferment, adding a second wild yeast. No raisins end up in the bread.
